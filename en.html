EMIA Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

EMIA is an Android app to keep and review your blood tests, donations and some health readings. This is particularly sensitive data, so EMIA is designed to keep it only on your device, unless you choose otherwise.

1. Who we are

EMIA is developed by Marcello Pandolfo (MP Studios).
For any question write to marcellopandolfo@gmail.com.

2. What data the app handles

  • Health data you enter: test values and reports, other medical documents, blood donations and their certificates, weight, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, blood glucose, menstrual cycle and symptom diary, pregnancy, diary appointments (including birthdays and babies' monthly milestones, worked out from dates of birth), therapies and recorded doses, allergies, chronic conditions, family history, vaccines, reference doctors and paediatricians.
  • Emergency contact and relationship: name and phone number of the person to notify and the relationship of family members to the account holder, if you choose to enter them.
  • Photos on the cards of people and pets, if you choose to add them.
  • Four-legged friends (EMIA Family Plus): profile, microchip, pedigree, vaccines, treatments, therapies, allergies, visits, tests, weight.
  • Optional personal details: name, date and place of birth, sex, height, blood group, for you and any family members you choose to add.

Entering this data is always voluntary.

3. Where it is stored

  • On your device, in an encrypted database. The encryption key is kept in Android's protected Keystore and never leaves the phone.
  • In the local backups the app creates on the device.

The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data. EMIA has no servers, requires no account and contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

4. When data leaves the device

Only by your explicit choice:

  • Google Drive backup (optional, with EMIA Family). If enabled, a copy is saved in your Google Drive, in a folder dedicated to EMIA. The app can only access files it creates (“drive.file” permission), not the rest of your Drive. Transfer is encrypted (HTTPS). You can turn it off at any time and delete the copies from your Drive. Google's privacy policy also applies to that service.
  • Family on several phones (optional, EMIA Family). If enabled, EMIA writes the data of the people (and pets) you choose to share into a file inside a folder you pick (for example a Google Drive folder shared with a family member), and reads the files the other phones leave in the same folder. The developer never sees or receives these files: who can read them depends on how you shared the folder.
  • Sharing and export (PDF, CSV, backup file, donation certificates by email). The app opens Android's share menu: you choose the app and the recipient.

5. Camera and documents

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a report. Value recognition runs on the device, without an Internet connection. Photos and PDFs stay in the app's private storage.

5a. Contacts and phone

If you choose “Pick from contacts” in a doctor's card, EMIA asks for permission to read your contacts and reads only the contact you pick (name, numbers, email, address, notes) to copy them into the card. It does not read the rest of your contacts and sends nothing off the phone. “Add to contacts” opens your contacts app with the contact already filled in: you decide whether to save it. The phone-call permission is used only to start a call when you tap the green button; without it, the dialer opens with the number already typed.

5b. Calendar and notifications

When you add an appointment to the calendar, EMIA opens the Calendar app with the event filled in: you save it. If you grant the calendar permission, EMIA adds and updates in the phone's calendar the document expiry dates entered on the cards (for example the identity card), with their alerts, and checks whether EMIA's appointments are already there: EMIA does not copy, store or send other events. Reminder notifications, including therapy doses, are only on if you turn them on, and they are generated on the phone. The “Alarms & reminders” permission, if you grant it, is used only to deliver dose and appointment reminders at the exact time. From EMIA's Settings you can open the phone page where EMIA can be excluded from battery saving, so that reminders are not delayed: the choice is yours and no data is sent.

5c. Emergency card on the lock screen

If you turn it on, EMIA shows a notification with emergency information on the lock screen (name, blood group, allergies, conditions, current therapies, doctor, emergency contact) and lets anyone open the emergency card without unlocking the phone. This information is therefore visible to anyone holding the phone: that is the purpose of the feature, designed for first responders. The card is read-only, gives no access to the rest of the phone or to other EMIA data, and can be turned off at any time in Settings.

6. Purchases

The yearly subscriptions (EMIA, EMIA Family, EMIA Family Plus, with a 60-day free trial) are handled entirely by Google Play: EMIA has no access to payment details. The app only receives Google's signed purchase confirmation, which it keeps on the device and in backups to restore the unlock.

7. Retention

Data stays on the device until you delete it. Deleting an item or uninstalling EMIA removes the data from the device. Copies saved elsewhere (Google Drive, shared files) remain under your control.

8. Your rights

You have full control of your data: you can view, edit, export and delete it at any time directly in the app. Since the developer holds no data about you, no request to us is needed; for any question you can still write to the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

9. Children

EMIA is intended for adults. An adult may enter data of family members, including minors, under their own responsibility.

10. Disclaimer

EMIA is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses. Talk to your doctor about any value outside the reference ranges.

11. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with their effective date.
